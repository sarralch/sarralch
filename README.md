# Hi, I'm Sarra Lachheb 👋

### AI engineer building retrieval-augmented and multimodal AI systems for healthcare, mobility, aviation and finance

I design AI systems that have to be right, not just fluent. That means grounding LLMs in
curated knowledge with hybrid retrieval, verifying their outputs with deterministic
checks, and measuring quality with proper evaluation. My work has been published at IEEE
and Springer venues.

- 🔬 **Focus:** RAG (hybrid search, reranking, evaluation), multimodal AI (OCR, vision, video)
- 🏥 **Domains:** healthcare decision support · vehicle intelligence · airport operations · document compliance
- 📫 **Reach me:** via [GitHub](https://github.com/sarralch)
